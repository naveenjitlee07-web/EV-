01 / THE CHALLENGE

Less searching. More doing.

Customer-facing teams need a clear path from finding a record to understanding its context and deciding what to do next.

01

Fragmented context

Bring customer details and recent activity into one view.

02

Too many decisions

Prioritize relevant actions instead of presenting every option at once.

03

Repetitive search

Combine structured filters with natural-language discovery.

Design hypotheses, not findings from validated user research.

02 / USER FLOW

A direct path to action.

Find customer→Review context→Choose action→Confirm
03 / WIREFRAMES

Three screens. One clear journey.

01. Discover

Persistent search and filters reduce navigation effort.

02. Understand

Group profile, activity and actions by priority.

03. Act

Keep suggested actions reviewable before confirmation.

04 / FINAL UI

Everything in its place.

A calm visual hierarchy, consistent status indicators and contextual actions support everyday customer operations.

05 / AI EXPERIENCE

Intelligence, with user control.

Ask AI

Find relevant information without memorizing filters.

Smart suggestions

Surface possible next steps with a reason.

Adaptive workspace

Recommend useful views while keeping layout changes optional.

✦ Ask AI Interactive concept
Choose a question to explore a sample response.

06 / REFLECTION

Designed for clarity, not complexity.

The concept prioritizes discoverability, contextual decision-making and transparent AI assistance. Next steps: validate task completion, accessibility and trust through usability testing.
